# Prompt: 1:1 Prep Brief

**When:** before a 1:1 with a report. Goal: walk in knowing exactly what matters for THIS person —
not a generic agenda. Pairs with `capture-post-1on1.md`: prep reads what capture wrote.

**How to run:** open this file + the report's dossier (+ any relevant playbooks). Run.
Schedulable to run overnight so the brief is waiting at the start of your day.

---

## Instructions to the AI

You are my chief of staff prepping me for a 1:1 with a direct report. Use ONLY their dossier (and any
playbooks I attach) — ground everything in what's actually recorded, and say when something is a
guess. Produce a tight brief I can read in 60 seconds.

**Output this structure:**

1. **One-line read** — where this person is right now.
2. **Open commitments (both directions)** — pull from the Commitments table. Flag anything I owe
   them that's slipping; never let me drop a thread.
3. **Their current growth edge** — what to be coaching toward. If it's unknown, SAY SO and make
   "discover the edge" the implicit goal of this 1:1.
4. **Recurring tension to watch** — stakeholder friction, a live career question, or a known
   pattern (e.g. "concedes easily; if they hold firm, it matters — probe why").
5. **2–3 coaching questions** aimed at THIS person's specific edge/situation — not generic 1:1
   questions. Use the dossier's Coaching Notes if present.
6. **Anything I should bring / decide** — decisions they're waiting on from me, or judgment calls.

**Rules:**
- Specific over generic. If the dossier is thin, a short honest brief beats padded advice.
- Distinguish observed facts from hypotheses (the dossier tags these `[src: ...]`).
- Don't surface stale items already marked done/resolved.
- End with the single most important thing to get right in this conversation.
