# Playbook: The 30-Second Update (synthesis, not summary)

**When:** "How's the project?" / "How's the team?" — from your boss, skip-level, or board. Any moment
you have ~30 seconds and 2–3 sentences to demonstrate command.

**Core idea:** A *summary* is an exhaustive retelling of everything that happened. A *synthesis* is
what truly matters. Vague sounds like you don't know what's going on; a laundry list sounds like you
can't tell what matters. Take a stand.

## The structure (2–3 sentences)
1. **State** — where things are now.
2. **Trend** — which way it's moving.
3. **Biggest problem** — the one that matters most.
4. **Solution + confidence** — what you'll do about it, and how sure you are it'll work.

## The rule that makes it land
**Proper nouns and numbers, not jargon.** Precision = command.
- ❌ "We have a sales problem."
- ✅ "We have a 10% decline in mid-market renewals."

## Example
> "Discovery's 33% done and accelerating. The biggest risk is the Acme integration — their API
> rate limits could blow our timeline. I'm scoping a caching layer this week; ~70% confident it
> closes the gap, and I'll know for sure by Friday."
