# Playbook: SCQA + Pyramid Principle

The McKinsey two-step. **SCQA structures your thinking; the Pyramid Principle structures your
delivery.** "The quality of your communication is a direct reflection of the quality of your
thinking" — so fix the thinking first.

## Step 1 — SCQA (get clear in your head)
- **Situation** — the stable context everyone agrees on. ("AI is pressuring our business.")
- **Complication** — what changed / the tension. ("Our security posture blocks using real
  customer data.")
- **Question** — the question the complication forces. ("How do we safely experiment?")
- **Answer** — your stance. ("Apply AI to our non-core business first; no sensitive data exposed.")

## Step 2 — Pyramid Principle (tell it)
Deliver in the *reverse* of how you discovered it — **lead with the answer.**
1. **Answer / headline** first — the *what* and the *why*.
2. **Arguments** next.
3. **Evidence** last.

> Headline: "Apply AI to non-core first — builds understanding and expands margin without risking
> the core." Punchline: "58% of costs sit in non-core; high confidence half is saveable via 4
> projects over 6 months."

## Start with the end in mind
Before any of this, answer: **"I win if WHO does WHAT?"** Name the audience and the specific action.
"Inform them" is not allowed — too vague to shape your language.

Balance **data** (makes them believe you — rigor) and **story** (makes them remember you — empathy).

## AI use
- "Restructure this using SCQA, then deliver it with the Pyramid Principle (answer first)."
- "Pressure-test this against SCQA/Pyramid — where do I bury the lede or skip evidence?"
