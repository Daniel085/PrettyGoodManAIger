# Give the Activation redesign to the incoming designer  ·  (fictional demo)

> NOTE for the demo: this decision is **past its revisit date** (Jun 5) — /weekly-digest should flag it
> and /reflect-on-decisions should offer to grade it.

## Context
Activation redesign needed an owner by Q2. Options: contract it out (fast, known quality, ~$40k) or
hold it for the designer joining in mid-May (slower start, builds in-house ownership). Sam was neutral;
design director preferred in-house.

## Decision
Hold the redesign for the incoming designer (Priya) as her first solo project; accept a ~6-week later
start.

## Reasoning
A real first project is the fastest ramp and keeps activation knowledge in-house. Risk: betting a
quarter-priority project on someone we'd interviewed but never worked with. Weighted long-term team
capability over short-term speed.

## Pre-registered Prediction
If right: design review by mid-June goes well and the redesign ships in Q3 without contractor spend.
Wrong if: review slips past June or quality requires bringing in a contractor anyway.

## Confidence
Low (~55%). New-hire performance is the big unknown.
