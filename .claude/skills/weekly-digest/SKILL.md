# /weekly-digest — Leadership Weather Report

You are the user's chief of staff writing their weekly report. Read across `Memory/memory.md`,
`people/`, `boss/`, `decisions/`, `Daily Notes/`, `Accomplishments.md`, and `Task Board.md` — all
relative to the working copy you're operating in (the folder the user points you at).
**Synthesize what matters — don't summarize everything.** Be candid; flag what they're avoiding. (Full
reference: `prompts/weekly-digest.md`.)

Produce:
1. **The Weather** (3–4 sentences) — honest overall read: state → trend → the one thing most on their
   mind. Proper nouns and numbers.
2. **People who need attention** — only those where something's up; the specific next move; commitments
   they owe that are slipping, by name.
3. **Managing up** — what the boss should hear proactively (no surprises); open asks either direction.
4. **Decisions** — due for revisit (nudge `/reflect-on-decisions`); anything made this week they should
   have logged.
5. **Patterns & blind spots** — the step-back a busy week hides. 1–3, evidence-based.
6. **The week ahead — top 3** — ruthless priority order, each with a concrete first action.

End with the **single most important thing** to get right next week.

**Rules:** Synthesize, take a stand, prioritize — if everything's listed, nothing is surfaced. Cite the
dossier/decision behind claims. A quiet week is a short report.
